# Product Backlog Prioritization & User Survey Analysis

## Introduction
In preparation for the first development cycle (Sprint 1), we conducted a survey with potential users of the EduCycle app (12 responses collected). The main goal was to validate the initial system requirements and determine their relative value, ensuring the Product Backlog ordering reflects the actual needs of the academic community. A 5-point Likert scale (1 = Not Important to 5 = Very Important) was used, allowing respondents to evaluate each feature independently.

## Feature Prioritization Results
Based on the collected data, we calculated the average importance score for each feature. The results dictated the following order for the Product Backlog, from highest to lowest priority:

1. **Search** (Average: 4.58 / 5.00)
2. **Filter & Sort** (Average: 4.50 / 5.00)
3. **Profile Customization** (Average: 4.17 / 5.00)
4. **In-App Transactions** (Average: 4.08 / 5.00)
5. **Shopping Cart** (Average: 3.25 / 5.00)
6. **Wishlist** (Average: 3.08 / 5.00)
7. **Chat** (Average: 2.67 / 5.00)
8. **Course Packs** (Average: 2.50 / 5.00)

## Newly Identified Requirements
In response to an open-ended question regarding missing features, users suggested several additions. These suggestions will be translated into new Product Backlog Items (PBIs) for future iterations:

* **Study Material Swaps:** A dedicated section for sharing and trading study notes, summaries, and exams, categorized by academic year and subject.
* **Student Verification:** Account validation using an institutional email address (e.g., university domain) to increase trust and security in transactions.
* **Tutoring Services:** A feature allowing students to offer or request academic tutoring.
* **Follow Users:** The ability to follow specific sellers' profiles to keep track of their new listings.

## Conclusion
Gathering user feedback proved essential for organizing the team's workflow. The data shows that discovery and navigation features (Search, Filter & Sort) are considered the most critical by users and must be the immediate focus of Sprint 1. Conversely, interaction features (Chat, Course Packs) received significantly lower scores, justifying their postponement to later iterations. The Product Backlog will be updated and ordered according to these metrics, alongside the new PBIs.
